1. Recover the requested artifact, audience, language and settled content. Complete new economic work through [ECONOMIST](../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md) steps 1–6, Economic priority and Requested work; preserve supplied propositions when editing and deliver the strongest derived conclusion.
