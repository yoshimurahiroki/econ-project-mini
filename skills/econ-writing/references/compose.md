# Compose economics prose

## Compose

Do not invent or independently write prose or materials: borrow and imitate at every scale, including a single word, label, short passage, translation, existing draft, paper, slide, Beamer and other material, in English and Japanese. First search for, obtain and read the relevant passage and its surrounding section, and inspect the actual page, slide, figure or table, from papers and slides in the five leading economics journals published in 2020 or later, major economics handbooks, review articles and guides, economics textbooks, or economics course materials (lecture notes, course slides and handouts). Map every output element to its actual source: content selection, argument structure, style, vocabulary, transitions, sentence endings, placement, design and appearance. Borrow and closely imitate the corresponding material. For Japanese, borrow corresponding Japanese originals; do not create a direct translation of English prose. Make only surgical adjustments necessary for established target facts, names, variables and symbols, population and period, numbers and units, citations, grammatical agreement, and the specified medium and 28 pt slide body text. Do not import the source study's empirical conditions or conclusions into the target. Record source-to-output locations and adjustments in the existing source, specification or work record; compare every element of the finished output with the original. If a reference passage is missing, return to search and reading; do not fill gaps with invented examples, free paraphrase, or independent argument structure, layout or design.

1. **Output: selected argument; consumer: draft.** Read `python scripts/route_registry.py read prose.common-output --format compact` from the runtime root. Select and closely imitate the actual argument structure of a matching original identified through the borrowing procedure, including its sequence of claims and evidence. Select supported results that change the mechanism, action ranking, allocation or economic magnitude, and fit them to that source structure through only the necessary adjustments. Retain scientific conditions and diagnostics in the source record. Recover the tested contrast or optimizing objective through [paper guidance](paper-workflow.md#compose-the-requested-section).
2. **Output: result-bound draft; consumer: artifact.** Retrieve quantities, definitions and source identities through existing code. Generate numerical text, tables and figures from the same saved result. The [research renderer](../../../harness/research.md) binds semantic keys, units and formats to producing input, definition, code and execution versions. Follow [PROSE's language rules](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#revise-for-the-requested-language) and [paragraph evidence and inference](paragraph-workflow.md#compose-the-paragraph), using [selected saved evidence](paragraph-workflow.md#command-entry-points) for inference consuming additional results. Apply the same source mapping to Abstracts, slides, summaries and whole papers; use paper guidance for architecture.
3. **Output: requested correspondence or revision; consumer: evidence review/response.** When requested, use the [evidence binder](../../../harness/research_evidence.py) on the selected manuscript: passages → original spans; statistics → result keys/units; equations/symbols → code, construction/calibration and residuals. Retain source quantities, assumptions, years and owner judgments with exact identities. Requested claim coverage/referee revision uses the [evidence contract](result-artifacts.md#claims-coverage-and-requested-re-review) and [manuscript-to-response sequence](paper-workflow.md#appendix-submission-and-referee-response): revise passages, assess current evidence, then prepare the response by the same borrowing procedure. Preserve earlier versions and reuse unchanged judgments/calculations.
4. **Output: inspected final artifact; consumer: delivery.** Apply PROSE's finish procedure and compare every element to its mapped original. A changed estimate or defining assumption updates affected numerical text and argument; a display-label change rebuilds displays from the saved calculation and follows the same source procedure. Keep execution links in the existing result record and deliver the supported economic argument.
