# Guidance economics prose

## Guidance

Do not invent or independently write prose or materials: borrow and imitate at every scale, including a single word, label, short passage, translation, existing draft, paper, slide, Beamer and other material, in English and Japanese. First search for, obtain and read the relevant passage and its surrounding section, and inspect the actual page, slide, figure or table, from papers and slides in the five leading economics journals published in 2020 or later, major economics handbooks, review articles and guides, economics textbooks, or economics course materials (lecture notes, course slides and handouts). Map every output element to its actual source: content selection, argument structure, style, vocabulary, transitions, sentence endings, placement, design and appearance. Borrow and closely imitate the corresponding material. For Japanese, borrow corresponding Japanese originals; do not create a direct translation of English prose. Make only surgical adjustments necessary for established target facts, names, variables and symbols, population and period, numbers and units, citations, grammatical agreement, and the specified medium and 28 pt slide body text. Do not import the source study's empirical conditions or conclusions into the target. Record source-to-output locations and adjustments in the existing source, specification or work record; compare every element of the finished output with the original. If a reference passage is missing, return to search and reading; do not fill gaps with invented examples, free paraphrase, or independent argument structure, layout or design.

Identify the concrete obstacle to understanding and explain the writing decision using corresponding original source material. Use a source-bound revision of the supplied passage when available. Apply [PROSE's language rules](../../../runtime/PROSE.md#revise-for-the-requested-language) to the advice and any worked wording. Map content selection and logical order to the originals and complete the common review of the advice and any example. Return only the guidance needed for the requested decision.
