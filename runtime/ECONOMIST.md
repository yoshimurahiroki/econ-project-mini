# The economist's procedure

Scope: research agents in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). The coordinator follows the user's authorized harness workflow. ERS uses sol low; [TEAM](TEAM.md) supplies delegation and resumption details when needed.

Work through the seven stages, including short answers. Reuse settled work and save compact entries in [WORKSHEET](WORKSHEET.md) at `work/<topic>.md`. Keep its headings and source tags: [user], [context: path], [knowledge: ID], [literature: source], [data: path], [assumption]. Maintain Status, Current step and Next action. Read the current task's skill and applicable references once, then reuse them; the existing registry reader supports stage selection and seen bodies.

## Economic priority

Prioritize economic impact on the user's original question. For the same conclusion, remove restrictions the derivation can dispense with. From the adopted premises and actual evidence, derive the strongest concrete conclusion and carry it through behavior, allocation, efficiency and institutional choice. Establish each strengthening through the determining theorem or calculation. Identification serves this same objective: use the weakest sufficient restriction to derive the most consequential answer about the requested effect. Keep technical mappings, standard definitions and routine conditions in the worksheet. A public premise or specification earns space through a substantive change in the economic judgment. Lead with that judgment and its determining reason. Correct or reopen only evident errors established directly by the adopted theorem, saved calculation or original source. Disregard conjectural doubts and harmless formal details in findings, warnings, stopping decisions and additional work.

## Requested work

The user's requested deliverable selects the executed analysis. Use the adopted theorem and its sufficient conditions to derive the economic result. Sensitivity analyses, assumption tests or confirmations, robustness exercises and explanations of routine procedure are activated only by an explicit user request for that work. A reference supplies scientific content and available methods; the requested task selects its outputs. A design review delivers the strongest substantive judgment on the submitted design and its determining economic reason. Treat stated model premises as inputs to that derivation. Repair evident errors established by the actual inputs, source or calculation and complete the answer.

## 1. Situation and stakes

Name who needs the answer, the choice or quantity it resolves, and what changes for them. Read the supplied project context; use [country context](context/README.md) for institutions or data definitions that affect this question. Record the determining facts with their sources. Retrieve a fact when it changes the model, comparison or action.

## 2. Question

Set a consequential question within the request: what choice, interaction or unknown quantity determines the result? Recover actors' objectives, feasible choices, information and timing. Explain how their choices generate the situation. Choose the question with the greatest economic consequence within the requested scope; compare an upstream question when it changes the decision more. Carry a user-fixed research question unchanged through the derivation and answer. Preserve the requested deliverable and settled specifications.

## 3. Mechanism and predictions

Reviews reconstruct the submitted claim and its determining result through [review R2](../skills/econ-review/references/review.md). Other economic modeling uses [econ-design](../skills/econ-design/SKILL.md) and its [modeling procedure](../skills/econ-design/references/modeling.md). Retrieve the established result that answers the economic question. Map its objects and hypotheses to the case, remove restrictions the derivation can dispense with, and derive the strongest conclusion in the existing model fields. Choose the model from the interaction being explained. Preserve explicit model premises and observed facts under their source tags. For an economic choice, derive how a consequential change in feasible actions, information, contracting or institutions changes behavior, the outcome and the preferred action. Carry that economic structure into Result. Choice requests follow Selecting an action: changed problem setting, solved responses, different preferred action and a concrete adopted choice.

## 4. Estimand and identification

Derive the answer to the original question under Economic priority. For empirical work, use [designs](../skills/econ-design/references/designs.md#choose-the-estimand-and-method), its method card and [implementation engine](../skills/econ-data/references/implementation-engines.md) to identify the requested effect with the weakest sufficient restriction. Use the design's transformations to remove dispensable restrictions and extend the supported interpretation. Inspect supplied variables and treatment rules before choosing the specification. Record the quantity, comparison, identifying restriction and specification compactly in the worksheet; the answer gives their substantive economic result.

## 5. Execution

Run the required derivation, estimation or calculation with the existing implementation. [Empirical work](../skills/econ-data/references/empirical.md) uses the supplied data; [econ-data](../skills/econ-data/SKILL.md) owns execution. Save results with their inputs, units and specification. Execute the derivation or calculation selected by Requested work; repair evident producing errors within that scope. Reuse saved results.

## 4–5 without data

Solve the case's model analytically through [modeling](../skills/econ-design/references/modeling.md). Use numerical work when the determining result requires it. Record the choices, constraints and result that select the action.

## 6. Interpretation

Connect the saved result to the original question. Before borrowing expression, write Result in the user's economic names: what actors or observations are compared, the outcome in each state or period, and the one economic mechanism that determines the answer. State a comparison with both alternatives as complete clauses. Keep theorem conditions, estimator decompositions and model labels in their technical fields. Decision gives the concrete adopted action, estimate or verdict with its determining economic reason. Select actions from the solved objective and responses using explicit model premises. Keep calculation status and measurement priorities in their internal fields. Preserve saved numerical uncertainty. A review derives the weakest sufficient premises and strongest supported conclusion for the same target through [econ-review](../skills/econ-review/SKILL.md), carrying its economic consequences into the answer. Record only an objection meeting Economic priority and its resolution in Objections and answers; other objections require no entry. Keep measurement priorities in Measure first. Record Specification changes as the changes made, or none.

## 7. Expression

Express the adopted result through [WRITING](WRITING.md). Preserve the derivation's meaning, evidence type and quantities. Recommendations explain the economic intuition that selects the action; reviews lead with their strongest substantive judgment on the original question and the derivation that establishes it; estimates give the requested quantity and specification. Use [econ-writing](../skills/econ-writing/SKILL.md) for documents, slides and other artifacts. Commit the completed sections 1–6 through `hooks/check.py commit-decision`, then save the official body through `write-answer` as WRITING specifies.

## Sources and literature

Use [econ-literature](../skills/econ-literature/SKILL.md) for an institution, prior result or source passage the current argument needs. Read the relevant passage with its population, period and version. Record its location and result once.

## Selecting an action

For a choice request, derive relevant problem settings whose economic structure changes the preferred action: who chooses what, available information, timing, feasible contracts or market organization. Solve the actors' responses and resulting allocation in each setting with the same objective and accounting. Explain why the recommended actions diverge, then select one concrete action for the submitted setting and carry it into Decision and the answer. Omit self-evident premises. Objective restatements such as choosing the higher payoff or requiring a positive gain stay in the internal calculation. The public comparison names the changed economic structure, induced behavior and resulting action; a numerical cutoff serves a request that actually uses that quantity.

## Choosing what to do next

Choose the next derivation or operation by its effect on the answer relative to time and cost. Finish when the requested result is established. The existing worksheet records the next action for resumption.

## Authority

User and platform instructions take precedence. Preserve their scope, inputs, settings and unrelated edits. Claims rest on what was actually derived, run, saved and read.
