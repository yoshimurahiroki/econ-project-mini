# Review procedure

Derive the weakest sufficient premises and strongest supported conclusion for the requested economic target. Review quality prioritizes economic impact, assumption economy, conclusion strength and deep economic implications. Apply [Economic priority](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md#economic-priority) to findings, objections and further work. Preserve the user's question, fixed specifications and actual evidence; reuse saved work in the existing worksheet.

## R1. Target

Record the artifact/version, original research question, actual submitted claims and requested use, with their locations or user source tags. Review those claims and the submitted design. Criticism addresses a concrete assertion or proposed operation from that material. Treat assistant-selected implementations as proposals to explain directly. Methodological warnings about an interpretation the user has never asserted stay in the internal derivation. Carry the original question through the review. Classify the claim as a description or coefficient, causal effect, prediction, theoretical result, recommendation, implementation or presentation. `assess` delivers the judgment; `assess+repair` also changes the authorized artifact.

## R2. Rebuild the economist's chain

Recover the [economist's chain](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md): question → target → supporting result → case evidence → conclusion. Improve that chain in one derivation pass:

1. Retrieve the theorem, method card or saved calculation that carries the central conclusion. Recover the decisive derivation for the original question.
2. Remove restrictions dispensable for that conclusion. Use the remaining premises and evidence to derive further substantive consequences. The determining theorem or calculation establishes both improvements.
3. Establish the gain: additional economically meaningful settings covered by the conclusion, or a stronger concrete statement derived from the adopted premises. Choose the most consequential supported answer and carry it through behavior, mechanism, efficiency or institutional choice. Conditions restated in new notation are internal proof work.
4. Record the strengthened answer and its determining derivation in Result and Decision, with source tags. State a relaxed premise publicly when it broadens the substantive conclusion. Repair evident errors at their producer under Economic priority and complete the answer.

Explicit model premises support their derived model conclusions; factual claims use case evidence. A new premise is an explicit change in the derivation. Requested targets and specifications determine the interpretation being strengthened.

## R3. Checks by kind of claim

Select the applicable row and determining reference. Inspect saved evidence first. Derive the requested result with saved evidence and the adopted theorem under ECONOMIST Requested work. Additional statistical exercises are selected by an explicit user request.

| Claim | Determining check |
|---|---|
| Description or coefficient | Derive the information established by the requested statistic, specification, sample, units and covariance. Strengthen its interpretation within that target and evidence. Causal claims use their identifying result. |
| Causal effect | Apply the [method card](../../econ-design/references/designs.md#choose-the-estimand-and-method) to establish the design's strongest substantive conclusion, allowing all selection and heterogeneity its theorem permits. Derive a claimed failure from a source-backed contradiction of the actual identifying restriction. State the economic gain from any relaxation. Exact restrictions, weights and measurement mappings are internal proof work. A requested estimate uses the [empirical procedure](../../econ-data/references/empirical.md) within Requested work. |
| Prediction | Match the target, available information, evaluation population and reported loss to the saved evaluation. |
| Theory or recommendation | Map the domain, hypotheses, feasible choices and objective to the proof or solved response. Derive the adopted choice and its economic consequences with the weakest sufficient hypotheses. For choice requests, follow ECONOMIST Selecting an action: derive contrasting problem settings, their responses and different preferred actions, then choose one concrete action. Public premises select that economic setting or establish a consequential result. |
| Structural or numerical result | Match parameters, moments, normalizations and code to the [adopted model](../../econ-design/references/designs.md#structural-estimation-and-counterfactuals). Inspect original equilibrium, feasibility or deviation residuals at the saved solution. Carry counterfactual changes through affected choices using consistent welfare accounting. |
| Numbers and sources | Trace decisive quantities to their sample, unit and saved result. Small rounding and presentation differences require no repair. For a registered evidence contract, `harness/research_evidence.py review` supplies selected original passages and bindings. |
| Code or pipeline | Trace the implicated producer and consumers, keys, joins, weights, sample and formula through the [implementation rules](../../econ-data/references/implementation.md). |
| Writing or exhibits | Preserve the established claim while applying [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md). Read changed rendered pages for a requested visual artifact using the [rendering procedure](../../econ-writing/references/artifact-writing.md). |

Complete the strongest economic conclusion answering the original question. An explicitly requested diagnostic is read against its own null and target under ECONOMIST Requested work.

## R4. Findings

Lead with the strongest substantive judgment and the decisive derivation. Explain the economic consequence gained through a weaker premise or stronger conclusion. A result already attaining that conclusion receives its affirmative derivation. For a demonstrated error meeting Economic priority, record:

```
Place and claim:
Evidence:        exact passage, failed restriction, calculation or valid counterexample
Consequence:     corrected quantity, interpretation or action
Cause:           source / transformation / inference / presentation / execution
Repair:          exact edit at the producer and affected outputs
```

Rank improvements first by economic impact, then by restrictions removed and conclusions gained. A finding identifies an evident error directly established by the adopted theorem, saved calculation or original source. Disregard conjectural doubts and harmless formal details in both the answer and work records. An assessment can return zero defects.

Repair the producing source, transformation, calculation or inference, then its affected consumers. Settle a contested finding against its original evidence. A referee response gives the substantive answer and exact revised locations through [result artifacts](../../econ-writing/references/result-artifacts.md#claims-coverage-and-requested-re-review).

## R5. Versions and decisions

Hold the target, inputs and criteria fixed in a comparison. Attribute changes to their actual sample, measurement, method or evidence. Reuse derivations whose target, evidence and review objective match. A user-authorized change in review objective starts a new assessment under its stated criteria. Reopen the affected claim after a determining change. Requested independent or blinded work follows [oversight](oversight.md).

## R6. Delivery

Use affirmative clauses to deliver the strongest substantive answer to the original question and its determining economic reason. Lead with the judgment and the economic reason. State what observations or choices are compared using the objects already named in the request. Select one decision-changing economic point per paragraph and express its comparison with complete clauses. A public criticism identifies the actual submitted assertion or operation it repairs. Remove technical commentary about hypothetical interpretations, and delete redundant topics rather than relocating them to new paragraphs. Complete the answer to the submitted design. A premise or specification earns public space through a substantive change in that judgment. Keep routine definitions, identifying conditions, scope accounting and computation in the worksheet. Express an evident error through the corrected result and its consequence. Choice answers explain the economic settings that produce opposing recommendations and adopt one action. A substantive improvement receives its exact proposed wording; an error receives its exact edit and corrected claim. Deliver the completed answer under Economic priority. `assess+repair` returns the corrected artifact and affected locations.

Complete one integrated derivation and expression pass. Select the verdict, its decisive economic reason and the consequence answering the original question. Delete sentences that only define a standard object, restate a routine condition or describe implementation. A further paragraph earns space through an additional substantive judgment. After a substantive change, revisit its affected inference and outputs, then deliver the strengthened answer under WRITING.
