# Explain economics prose

## Explain

1. Read a paper through [paper reading](../../econ-literature/references/paper-reading.md); recover its question, mechanism, comparison and results with population and units. For a theory, institution or method use the corresponding economic work in [ECONOMIST](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md).
2. State the answer first in the question's economic names. For comparisons, name both observations or choices and the outcome being compared. Select one economic point per paragraph and express it with complete clauses. Borrow source expression under [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md) for that plain meaning; use the existing finish pass to remove extra methodological commentary. Match its length to the request.
