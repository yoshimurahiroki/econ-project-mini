# Expression review pointer

1. **Source first → located passage → composer.** [WRITING](../runtime/WRITING.md) owns the source-borrowing procedure for every element of English and Japanese prose, papers, materials, slides and Beamer. The [recurring-pattern table](prose_rules.json) maps locators to its steps; `python harness/local_checks.py scan-text --input FILE --dictionary quality/prose_rules.json` returns source positions.
The existing write-answer and research Stop handler enforce dictionary rules marked block for negative predicates, contrasts, prerequisite deferrals, routine reporting announcements and regulated expressions. Regulated families include centrality labels, linking verbs, retention verbs, linear-response phrases, separate-classification labels interpretive treatment/definition formulas causal-interpretation support framing, vague aiming, estimation-role metaphors identification-dispute labels generic placement/entry predicates and abstract specification labels, with Japanese inflections and English equivalents. A marked passage returns to the writer as an affirmative adopted action, quantity or exact correction.

2. **Revised artifact → final review.** Apply the complete [finish procedure](../runtime/WRITING.md) to meaning and rhetorical function, including passages beyond the listed locators. A locator or a record supplements reading the source.
