# Compose economics prose

## Compose

1. Complete [ECONOMIST](../../../runtime/ECONOMIST.md) steps 1–6; compose its adopted question, mechanism, comparison, results and decision.
2. Follow [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md): retrieve the original paragraph for the adopted judgment, then look up predicates and noun–verb phrases while composing. Copy source vocabulary, sentences and clause structure into the working draft. Batch several queries in the existing exemplar tool and reuse the examples. Papers and notes use [paper workflow](paper-workflow.md); saved-result exhibits use [result artifacts](result-artifacts.md).
