# Building and simulating a model, step by step

Use existing knowledge to resolve the economic question. Save the determining reasoning in the worksheet and reuse settled content.

## Step 1. Decision and actors

State the objective, feasible choices, information and timing. Identify the choices and interactions that generate the observed situation and the question whose answer changes the decision.

## Step 2. Candidate models

Retrieve the established result for that interaction with `knowledge.py search`, then `knowledge.py plan UNIT-ID --read --focus`. Read its objects, hypotheses and conclusion. Reuse the returned body and consult a prerequisite when the derivation uses it.

## Step 3. Fit to the case and order the ladder

Map the result's objects to the case. Record observed facts and explicit model premises under their source tags. Choose the smallest structure that represents the actors' consequential choices and establishes the requested result. Trace each restriction to its use in the derivation and remove redundant restrictions; use logical implication and wider applicability to judge weaker premises. A changed hypothesis uses its corresponding result or a direct derivation.

## Step 4. Write the model

Write objectives, choices, constraints, information and the solution concept. Apply the mapped result and derive the determining optimality condition, equilibrium, identifying equality or deviation argument. Carry the induced behavior through allocation, prices or participation to efficiency, distribution and institutional choice as needed to solve the requested decision. Record the mathematical step establishing the conclusion in Key condition and its implication in Result.

For a choice request, follow ECONOMIST Selecting an action. Change the game's actors or actions, information, timing, feasible contracts or market organization where it changes the preferred action. Derive behavior and outcomes in the relevant settings using the same objective and accounting. Explain the economic force behind the opposing recommendations and record one concrete selected action in Decision. Keep self-evident premises and objective-restatement boundaries out of the public answer.

## Step 5. Code and simulation

Reuse closed forms, saved results and existing solvers. Numerical work serves the determining comparison. The existing `skills/econ-design/templates/simulate_model.py` supports its covered numerical models. Save input definitions, units, economic premise, response and result. Execute sequentially.

## Step 6. Read the results

Connect the solved result to the question and carry its economic intuition and concrete action into the answer. Apply ECONOMIST Economic priority and Requested work to objections and further calculations; record only an evident error and its resolution. Measurement priorities stay in Measure first. Use [WRITING](../../../runtime/WRITING.md) to express the supported result.
